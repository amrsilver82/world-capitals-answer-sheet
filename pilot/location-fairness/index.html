What are we testing?

The goal is to distinguish geographic knowledge from tiny touch targets. The seven tiny-country cases include microstates and Pacific islands. Chad and Bhutan serve as ordinary-map controls. Each island group accepts any of its mapped islands when evaluating pins.

The grid has uniform 20° × 20° cells. The preview does not reveal the correct cell until you submit. Polygon distances are approximate and are only used to compare methods. No country is excluded or officially scored in this test. GitHub stores only experimental attempt events and explicit Next actions in a separate private Issue.
