Côte d’Ivoire vs Ireland · Monaco vs Indonesia

1) Côte d’Ivoire vs Ireland

Same three colours, opposite order.

Côte d’Ivoire 🇨🇮

Left → right: ORANGE · WHITE · GREEN

Orange is at the flagpole/left side. Green is on the right.

Ireland 🇮🇪

Left → right: GREEN · WHITE · ORANGE

Green is at the flagpole/left side. Orange is on the right.

Memory trick: Côte d’Ivoire starts with ORANGE; Ireland starts with GREEN.

2) Monaco vs Indonesia

The colour pattern is the same. The reliable visual difference is the flag proportion.

Monaco 🇲🇨

Official proportion: 5:4 (width:height)

Monaco is more square/compact.

Indonesia 🇮🇩

Official proportion: 3:2 (width:height)

Indonesia is wider/longer.

Memory trick: Monaco = squarer. Indonesia = wider. Do not rely on the shade of red because displays and image sources can vary.
